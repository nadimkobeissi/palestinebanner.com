# Donation options — Palestine Banner

> Official donation links for organizations providing medical care and humanitarian aid to Palestinians, including in Gaza.

Canonical page: https://palestinebanner.com/click/

## Palestine Children’s Relief Fund (PCRF)

Medical care for children and relief for families. Its donation page includes a Gaza Relief and Recovery fund.

[Donate to PCRF](https://www.pcrf.net/donate).

## Medical Aid for Palestinians (MAP)

Medical aid, nutrition, and mental health support for Palestinians in Gaza, the West Bank, and refugee communities in Lebanon.

[Donate to MAP](https://www.map.org.uk/how-to-help/donate/).

## UN Agency for Palestine Refugees (UNRWA)

Food, healthcare, education, and emergency assistance for Palestine refugees in Gaza and across the region.

[Donate to UNRWA](https://donate.unrwa.org/).

## About these links

Some funds support work beyond Gaza. Check the appeal and choose a fund on the organization’s website.

Palestine Banner does not collect donations or receive fees. It is not affiliated with these organizations. Donations are made through each organization’s official website.
